ABOUT ME

DJ based in Stockholm, Sweden

I play and mix a variety of music as a hobby, ranging from disco-era hits all the way to modern club bangers.

The majority of the music I mix is electronic-based, primarily house, techno and trance. My favourite styles include UK garage, hard house and bounce techno, with a lot of inspiration drawn from the 90s rave scene.

New mixes will be posted here to give you an idea of what this type of music is all about.

I'm looking to take the next step in my DJ journey and turn my passion into a profession. That's why I'm now available for hire for your upcoming events, playing anything from mainstream EDM to alternative house.

The music I can bring to your dancefloor includes, but is not limited to, techno, eurodance, synth-pop, disco and Swedish pop. You name it!

If you're interested in booking me, would like to know more about me, or have any other questions, please feel free to get in touch via the email below.
